Supplier role

What reinforcing mesh suppliers should help you solve

Reinforcing mesh, also called reo mesh, gives concrete additional tensile strength and helps control cracking in slabs, pavements, driveways, footings and other concrete elements. A good supplier does more than sell sheets. They help you source the right mesh, understand the related accessories, and get everything to site in a sequence that supports the construction program.

For projects that need a mix of products, Form Direct's Reinforcing & Accessories range is a practical starting point. It covers steel mesh sheets, reinforcing bars, trench mesh, fibre reinforcement, bar chairs, dowels and jointing products used across residential, commercial and civil work.

Start with the type of concrete mesh you need

Before comparing reo suppliers, match the product category to the job. Start with the engineering drawings, then confirm whether the project calls for SL mesh, RL mesh, trench mesh, reinforcing bar, or a combination of products and accessories.

A good supplier should be able to talk through the order as a complete package: mesh type, sheet quantity, related bars, chairs, delivery access and timing.

Site logistics

Why delivery and scheduling matter as much as the mesh

Reinforcing mesh often looks straightforward until the delivery window is missed. A slab pour may require mesh sheets, trench mesh, starter bars, bar chairs, dowels, jointing materials, concrete consumables and site setup products to arrive in the right order. If one part is missing, the site can lose hours or days.

For contractors managing multiple pours, delivery planning can be the difference between a clean morning start and an expensive delay.

Quick answers

FAQs about reinforcing mesh suppliers

What is the difference between reo mesh and trench mesh?

Reo mesh is a broad term for reinforcing mesh used in concrete. Trench mesh is a narrower reinforcing product designed for footings, beams and structural bases.

What is wire mesh for a concrete slab?

Wire mesh for a concrete slab usually refers to welded reinforcing mesh placed within the concrete to help control cracking and improve tensile performance.

Which trench mesh size should I use?

Use the trench mesh size specified by the engineer or slab design. Do not substitute a trench mesh size without approval.
